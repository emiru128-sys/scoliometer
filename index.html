Where the levels are

Viewed from behind. The helper feels for the landmarks on the right, slides the phone through a zone, then taps that zone and logs the peak.

How to measure
  1. Person measured: feet together, knees straight, bend forward from the hips until the back is level with the floor. Arms hang, palms together, head relaxed.
  2. Helper stands behind, eyes level with the back, and lays the phone flat across it at the neck bump: screen up, long edge across the spine, top of the phone pointing to the patient's left.
  3. Slide slowly down the spine. The peak updates by itself.
  4. After each zone, tap that level, then Log peak. Four zones, top to bottom. Or use the guided session, which walks you through it.
  5. Same time of day and the same bend depth each session, so days compare.

7° or more at any level is the usual screening cut-off for an X-ray. The shaded band on the dial marks it.
