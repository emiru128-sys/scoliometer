Peak —

How to measure
  1. Someone stands behind you. You bend forward, feet together, knees straight, arms hanging, until your back is level with the floor.
  2. They lay the phone flat across your back at the top, screen up, long edge across the spine, top of the phone pointing to your left.
  3. They slide it slowly down the spine. The peak updates by itself.
  4. After each region, tap that level, then Log peak. Four levels, top to bottom.
  5. Same time of day and the same bend depth each session, so days compare.

7° or more at any level is the usual screening cut-off for an X-ray. The shaded band on the dial marks it.
